02
Health Data
Access, collection & usage of sensitive health information
⚠️ Sensitive Data Notice
Health data is classified as sensitive personal information. We collect it only with explicit parental or guardian consent, and use it solely for student safety and welfare.

We may collect and store the following types of health-related data for students:

  • ✓Medical Conditions: Chronic conditions or disabilities relevant to school participation (e.g., asthma, diabetes)
  • ✓Allergies: Known food, environmental, or medication allergies to ensure student safety
  • ✓Medications: Prescription medications that need to be administered during school hours
  • ✓Emergency Medical Notes: Instructions from parents or guardians for emergency situations

How this health data is used:

  • →To inform teachers and staff of conditions requiring special attention or accommodation
  • →To respond appropriately during medical emergencies on school premises
  • →To coordinate care with school nurses or health officers
🚫 Health Data Is Never
Sold, shared with advertisers, used for marketing, or disclosed to any third party not directly responsible for student care and welfare.

Data Retention
How long we store your information

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law:

ACT
Active Enrollment Period All student academic, attendance, and communication data is retained for the full duration of active enrollment.
3 YR
Post-Enrollment — Academic Records Grades, transcripts, and performance records are retained for up to 3 years after a student leaves, for official reference purposes.
1 YR
Post-Enrollment — Contact & Health Data Personal contact details and health records are retained for 1 year after enrollment ends, then securely deleted.
90D
Device & Usage Logs Technical and usage logs are retained for 90 days for security and diagnostic purposes, then automatically purged.
ℹ️ Legal Override
Where retention is required by applicable education law or regulatory mandate, we may retain certain data for longer periods to remain compliant.
06
Data Deletion
How to request removal of your data

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. Here is how the process works:

  • ①Submit a Request: Email abhishekborpa@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request," including your full name, role, and associated school name.
  • ②Identity Verification: We will verify your identity within 5 business days to protect against unauthorized requests.
  • ③Processing: Once verified, your data will be permanently deleted from our active systems within 30 days.
  • ④Confirmation: You will receive written confirmation once deletion is complete.
⚠️ Exceptions to Deletion
Some data may be retained even after a deletion request if required by law (e.g., official academic records mandated by education authorities). We will inform you of any exceptions at the time of your request.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)
Special protections for users under 13
🛡️ Children Under 13
This application may be used by children under 13 years of age. We apply the highest level of data protection to all minor users in compliance with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

In compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy, we:

  • ✓Require verifiable parental or guardian consent before collecting any personal data from children under 13
  • ✓Do not display behavioural advertising to children under 13
  • ✓Do not collect more data than is strictly necessary for educational functionality
  • ✓Allow parents and guardians to review, correct, or delete their child's data at any time
  • ✓Store children's data with enhanced encryption and strictly limited access controls

Parents or guardians wishing to review or delete their child's data may contact us at privacy@yourschoolapp.com.

Data Security
How we protect your information

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • 🔒All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS)
  • 🔒Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • 🔒Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • 🔒Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments are conducted
  • 🔒Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available for all administrator accounts

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities within the timeframe required by applicable law.
